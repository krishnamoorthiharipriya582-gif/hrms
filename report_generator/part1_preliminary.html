SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a collection of 17 global goals adopted in 2015 by all United Nations Member States as a universal call to action to end poverty, protect the planet, and ensure peace and prosperity for all by 2030.

The Employee Onboarding and HR Management System aligns directly with three vital Sustainable Development Goals:

Questions Answer Details & Alignment
Which SDGs does the project directly address? SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth — By automating statutory wage calculations, ensuring timely disbursement, and enforcing legal labor protections (EPFO, ESIC, Gratuity).

SDG 9: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure — Upgrading enterprise HR operational infrastructure with cloud microservices, digital document KYC, and automated compliance filing.

SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities — Enforcing unbiased compensation structures, policy-driven leave allowances, transparent appraisals, and non-discriminatory hiring.
What strategies or actions are being implemented to achieve these goals? • Automated gross-to-net Indian statutory payroll engine computing PF (12%), ESIC (0.75%/3.25%), and TDS.
• Digital paperless onboarding with automated credential and document KYC verification.
• Objective multi-level performance appraisals (PMS) with cascaded goals to eliminate human bias.
• Geofenced attendance with 09:15 AM grace cutoff ensuring fair labor tracking.
How is progress measured and reported in relation to the SDGs? • 100% statutory payroll compliance rate with zero calculation discrepancies.
• Audit trail logging capturing 100% of PII mutations and approvals under DPDP Act 2023.
• 85% reduction in onboarding onboarding turnaround time (from 14 days to 48 hours).
• Elimination of paper dossiers through 100% cloud KYC storage.
How were these goals identified as relevant to the project's objectives? Fair wages, transparent workplace policies, and reliable social security contributions are foundational pillars of Decent Work (SDG 8) and organizational equity (SDG 10).
Are there any partnerships or collaborations in place to enhance this impact? Designed with open API standards for seamless integration with the EPFO Electronic Challan cum Return (ECR) portal, ESIC Monthly Return Gateway, and Income Tax TRACES / Form 24Q filing systems.
